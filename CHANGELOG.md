## 0.2.2 · The Lounge in one screen

Hotfix · 2026-09-14

The bet button moved up beside the amount, and the coin waits to land before it says how it went.

### Changed

- The loss sting was mastered about 13 dB louder than the win chime. It sits at the same level now, in the Lounge and on a ratioed post, and the flop sting comes down with it
- The bet button sits beside the amount, so nothing needs a scroll to bet. Both tables fit one laptop screen: the wheel keeps its odds beside the seed and the coin sits next to its own line

### Fixed

- The coin flip played its win or loss sound the moment you pressed the button, 0.9 s before the coin landed. The press is now a soft toss and the result sounds when the face shows
- Same on the wheel: the sampler ticks while the seed scrambles, the payoff sounds when it settles, and the reel turns during the scramble instead of after it
- The bank line, the pity pips, the pot and the win flash all moved the moment you bet. They hold still now until the coin or the seed lands

## 0.2.0 · The Latent Lounge

Release · 2026-09-14

Two tables, a breaker that means it, and people running your workflows.

### Added

- The Latent Lounge, which is Seed Roulette with a clearer name and a second table
- Coin flip: your side pays double at 48%, the Comfy side takes the stake at 52%
- A bet slider and a number box that always agree, plus Min, Half, Double, 25%, 50% and All in
- Citizens: publish a workflow and invented players start running it, paying you the usual 5% royalty
- A board on /hub showing who ran what, what it paid and how much interest is left

### Changed

- Bets are priced in credits. No more 30s / 1m / 5m presets and no spin cooldown
- Pressing S has an eight second cooldown. Autosave is untouched

### Balance

- The wheel pays back 0.954 on the credit, down from 1.038 when the cooldown was doing the work
- A tripped breaker now stops every rig instead of scaling income down. Clicks still pay
- A published workflow pays about 43 seconds of income over 43 minutes, then goes cold
